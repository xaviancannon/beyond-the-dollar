Economics

Understanding how people, businesses, and governments make choices about limited resources.

📈 Supply & Demand

Supply and demand are two fundamental concepts in economics. They help explain how prices and quantities are determined in markets.

Supply

Supply refers to how much of a good or service producers are willing and able to sell at different prices.

Demand

Demand refers to how much of a good or service consumers are willing and able to buy at different prices.

💵 Inflation

Inflation is an increase in the overall level of prices for goods and services over time.

When prices rise, the purchasing power of money generally decreases, meaning the same amount of money can buy fewer goods and services.

🏦 Interest Rates

Interest rates affect the cost of borrowing money and the return people can receive from saving or lending money.

Changes in interest rates can influence consumer spending, business investment, saving, and other economic activity.

🌎 GDP & Unemployment

GDP

Gross Domestic Product, or GDP, measures the monetary value of final goods and services produced within an economy during a particular period.

Unemployment

The unemployment rate measures the percentage of people in the labor force who are unemployed and actively looking for work.
