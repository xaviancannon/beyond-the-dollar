Global Economy

Understanding how countries, businesses, and financial markets are connected.

🌎 What Is the Global Economy?

The global economy is the network of economic activity that connects countries around the world.

Countries trade goods and services, exchange currencies, invest in one another, and depend on international supply chains.

🚢 International Trade

International trade occurs when countries buy and sell goods and services from one another.

Exports

Exports are goods and services sold to buyers in other countries.

Imports

Imports are goods and services purchased from other countries.

💱 Exchange Rates

An exchange rate describes how much one country's currency is worth relative to another country's currency.

Exchange rates can affect international trade, travel, investments, and the prices of imported goods.

🔗 Economic Interdependence

Countries are economically interconnected. A change in one part of the world can sometimes affect businesses, consumers, and markets in other countries.

Global supply chains are one example of this interdependence. A product may be designed in one country, use materials from several others, and be assembled somewhere else.

📊 Comparative Advantage

Comparative advantage is the idea that countries can benefit from specializing in goods and services they can produce at a lower opportunity cost.

This concept helps explain why countries trade with one another rather than producing everything themselves.
