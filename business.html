Business

Understanding how companies create value, make money, compete, and grow.

🏢 How Businesses Make Money

Businesses provide goods or services that customers are willing to pay for. Revenue is the money a business receives from selling those goods or services.

Revenue vs. Profit

Revenue is the money a business brings in. Profit is what remains after the business pays its expenses.

💼 Business Models

A business model describes how a company creates value for customers and generates revenue.

Examples

Companies can make money through product sales, subscriptions, advertising, licensing, transaction fees, and many other methods.

⚔️ Competition

Businesses often compete with one another for customers. Competition can influence prices, product quality, innovation, and the choices available to consumers.

🚀 Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship involves identifying opportunities and creating or developing businesses to pursue them.

Entrepreneurs often take financial and business risks while trying to create something customers value.
