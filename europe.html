Europe

Exploring European economies, trade, currencies, history, and development.

🇪🇺 The European Union

The European Union is a political and economic partnership between European countries.

Its members cooperate on areas such as trade, economic policy, and the movement of goods and people.

💶 The Euro

The euro is the common currency used by many European Union countries.

Using a common currency can make trade and travel between participating countries easier.

🚢 European Trade

European countries trade extensively with one another and with countries outside Europe.

Trade connects European businesses and consumers to markets around the world.

📊 European Economies

Europe contains economies of many different sizes and structures.

Factors such as population, natural resources, industries, technology, trade, and government policy can influence how an economy develops.

🏛️ Economic History

European economic development has been shaped by centuries of trade, industrialization, technological change, wars, financial crises, and international cooperation.

Understanding this history helps explain many of the economic relationships that exist in Europe today.
